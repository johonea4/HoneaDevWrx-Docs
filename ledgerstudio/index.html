LedgerStudio • User Guide

Run Your Business Offline on Android & Windows.

Create sharp quotes & invoices, track billable job time, record deductible expenses & business mileage, capture client signatures, and move encrypted backups safely between devices.

Backup & Transfer (.lsvault) Support & Restore Purchases

1. Getting Started & Demo Workspace

When you open LedgerStudio for the first time, it loads realistic contractor demo records so you can immediately explore charts, quotes, invoices, and PDF output.

  • Sample demo records do not count toward the 5-document Starter limit.
  • Whenever you are ready for your live business, open Vault & Tax → Erase All Local Data to start with a clean ledger, then configure your company details under Business Profile & PDF Branding.

2. Cashflow Dashboard & Estimated Tax Reserve

The Dashboard tracks Collected YTD Revenue, Net Operating Profit, Estimated Tax Reserve (configurable under Vault & Tax), Safe-to-Spend Net Income, open vs. overdue receivables, and a 6-month revenue vs. cost chart.

3. Quotes, Invoices, Payments & Digital Signatures

  • Create Quotes or Invoices: Add itemized quantities, unit prices, tax %, percentage or flat discounts, and payment terms.
  • Convert Quotes in 1 Tap: Converting an approved quote into an invoice preserves all line items without using another Starter document slot.
  • Record Payments: Log partial deposits or final payments with cash, check, ACH, card, or transfer notes. Balance due and Paid status update automatically.
  • Digital Signature Capture (Pro): On the document detail page, have your customer sign directly on your touchscreen or mouse to embed their vector signature on the exported PDF.

4. Operations: Live Job Timer, Expenses & Mileage

  • Live Stopwatch Timer: Start/stop active work sessions, save billable hours at your configured hourly rate, and import unbilled time directly onto a new or draft invoice.
  • Deductible Expenses & Mileage: Log fuel, materials, equipment, software, subcontractors, and business trips (mi or km) to feed real-time profit and Schedule C tax totals.

5. Encrypted Cross-Device Backup & Transfer (.lsvault)

Because LedgerStudio stores nothing in the cloud, you use portable .lsvault files (encrypted with PBKDF2-SHA256 100,000 iterations + AES-256-GCM) to back up your data or move between your Android phone and Windows PC:

  1. Open Vault & Tax → AES-256-GCM Encrypted Vault (.lsvault).
  2. Enter a strong passphrase (at least 6 characters) and tap Export Encrypted Backup.
  3. Send the .lsvault file via USB, Quick Share, local network share, or email/Drive to your other device.
  4. On the destination device, enter the same passphrase and tap Restore .lsvault File.
