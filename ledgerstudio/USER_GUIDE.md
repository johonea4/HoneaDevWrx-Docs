# LedgerStudio User Guide — HoneaDevWrx

**LedgerStudio** is a 100% offline business, invoicing, time tracking, expense, mileage, and encrypted tax backup studio for **Android** (`com.honeadevwrx.ledgerstudio`) and **Windows 10/11** (`34743JMHonea.LedgerStudio` · Store ID `9N5J73G62VMV`).

## Quick Workflow
1. **Explore Demo Data or Start Clean:** Demo sample records do not use up your 5 free Starter document slots. Tap **Vault & Tax -> Erase All Local Data** when ready for live business records.
2. **Configure Business Branding:** Set business name, email, currency, distance unit (`mi`/`km`), tax %, hourly rate, mileage rate, tax reserve %, and PDF accent color in **Vault & Tax**.
3. **Quotes -> Invoices:** Create estimates and convert approved quotes to invoices in one tap. Record deposits/payments anytime.
4. **Operations:** Track live job timer sessions, convertible to invoice line items in 1 tap, alongside deductible expenses and business mileage.
5. **Cross-Device `.lsvault` Backup:** Export an AES-256-GCM encrypted `.lsvault` file from **Vault & Tax**, move it to your phone or PC, and restore using the same passphrase.

## Editions & Support
