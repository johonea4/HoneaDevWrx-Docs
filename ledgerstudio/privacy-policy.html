Privacy Policy for LedgerStudio

1. Zero Personal or Financial Data Collection

LedgerStudio is engineered to run 100% offline with no backend servers. HoneaDevWrx does not collect, access, transmit, rent, or sell any personal, business, or usage data.

  • Stored Strictly On Your Device: Your business profile, tax rates, client contact details, quotes, invoices, payment logs, client signatures, billable timer entries, deductible expenses, and mileage records reside solely inside your local device SQLite database (ledgerstudio_v1.db3).
  • No Tracking SDKs: LedgerStudio contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero advertising networks, zero social trackers, and zero external crash telemetry uploaders.

2. AES-256-GCM Encrypted Vault Backups (.lsvault)

If you use the optional Pro backup feature to export a .lsvault archive, LedgerStudio compresses and encrypts your local database entirely on your device using a passphrase you supply via PBKDF2-SHA256 (100,000 iterations) key derivation and AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption before opening your operating system's file picker or share sheet. HoneaDevWrx never receives your passphrase or backup files.

3. Device Permissions & Store Billing

  • File Export & Share Sheet: Requested only when you explicitly tap Export PDF, Export CSV, Save .lsvault, or Restore .lsvault.
  • Google Play Billing / Microsoft Store IAP: LedgerStudio is free to install with an optional one-time in-app unlock (ledgerstudio_pro_lifetime, $24.99 USD). Purchase checkout, tax handling, and receipt verification are processed directly by Google LLC (Google Play) or Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft Store) under their respective privacy policies. LedgerStudio writes an HMAC-SHA256 integrity-verified entitlement flag to local device preferences so Pro features continue functioning offline.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because HoneaDevWrx stores no copy of your data, you have complete control over retention and deletion at any time:

  • Open Vault & Tax → Erase All Local Data inside LedgerStudio to wipe all business records immediately.
  • Uninstalling LedgerStudio from Android or Windows removes the application's private database and preferences from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

LedgerStudio is a business bookkeeping and invoicing utility intended for adults (18+) and commercial professionals, and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Policy Updates & Contact

Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be posted at this URL with an updated Effective Date. For privacy or support questions, email honeadevwrx@outlook.com.
