CraftLedger Support & Frequently Asked Questions

Need direct assistance? Email honeadevwrx@outlook.com with your device type (Android or Windows) and a brief description of your question.

How do I restore my CraftLedger Pro purchase on a new device?

Sign in to Google Play (Android) or Microsoft Store (Windows) using the same store account that purchased CraftLedger Pro (craftledger_pro_lifetime), open CraftLedger → Vault & Tax → Pro Studio → Restore Store Purchase. Because Google Play and Microsoft Store handle platform entitlements separately, Pro licenses are restored within each storefront ecosystem.

How do I move my invoices and clients between Windows and Android?

Open Vault & Tax → AES-256-GCM Encrypted Vault (.clvault), enter a passphrase (min 6 characters), and tap Export Encrypted Backup. Transfer the generated .clvault file to your target phone or PC, enter the same passphrase, and tap Restore .clvault File.

I forgot my .clvault passphrase — can HoneaDevWrx reset it?

No. Backups are encrypted locally on your device using PBKDF2-SHA256 + AES-256-GCM without any cloud key escrow. Store your vault passphrase in a trusted password manager.

Do sample demo quotes and invoices count against the 5 free documents?

No. Sample contractor records loaded on first launch (and quotes you convert into invoices) are excluded from the 5-document Starter limit.
